Information we collect

How we use information

We use contact information to reply to enquiries, prepare proposals and deliver services. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except where required to deliver a service you have requested or where the law requires it.

Data on client projects

For client engagements we handle project data under the terms of each client agreement. Data collected by products we build for clients is governed by that client's own privacy policy, not this one.

Retention

We keep enquiry records only as long as needed to handle them and to meet legal or accounting obligations. You may ask us to delete correspondence about your enquiry at any time.

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details on our contact page.

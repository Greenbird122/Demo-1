Home / Services / SaaS development

Products, not projects.

Subscription platforms that take on customers without taking on rewrites.

What we build

  • New SaaS products — first architecture to paying customers.
  • Multi-tenant platforms with accounts, roles and permissions.
  • Billing and subscriptions — cards and mobile money, first-class.
  • Admin dashboards, reporting and operational tools.

How we approach it

A SaaS product is a promise you keep every month. Tenancy, billing, onboarding and monitoring go into the first release — not after it.

That costs a little more up front. It saves a rewrite later. We build with local payment realities in mind: mobile money and cards are first-class citizens here.

Recent work

Mkulima Alert — freemium weather service with mobile-money billing designed into the platform.

Tulia Tag — consumer platform with accounts, roles and real-time features.

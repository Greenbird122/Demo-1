Apps people keep using.

Built for real conditions first — weak networks, small screens, real users. Polish layered on top.

What we build

  • Customer apps — ordering, booking, accounts, support.
  • Field tools — data capture, inspections, delivery and logistics.
  • Offline-first apps that keep working when the network doesn't.
  • Apps paired with hardware — phones talking to sensors and devices.

How we approach it

Most apps fail for boring reasons: slow on older phones, useless without internet, painful to update. We design against those first.

Our field deployments — agriculture, logistics, community programmes — taught us the order: real users before demo rooms.

Recent work

Tulia Tag — bag tracking and recovery with live maps, secure codes and face verification.

Mkulima Alert — weather alerts and advisories on any phone, no internet needed.

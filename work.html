Every product here began as a client's idea. We designed it, built it and shipped it. All of them run in the real world today.

Mkulima Alert

Web platform, IoT and data

Daily weather forecasts for Kenyan smallholder farmers — by SMS, in local languages, on basic phones. Data from three meteorological sources, analysed automatically, delivered as plain farming advice.

Built: data integration, AI analysis, SMS delivery, local-language messaging

Live: mkulima-alert-1x96.vercel.app

"I don't have Google. I just need to know — will it rain tomorrow?"

Tulia Tag

Mobile and web application

Smart bag tracking and recovery. Owners watch live location, generate secure recovery codes, and chat with the finder — who verifies identity by face before touching the recovery tools.

Built: live tracking, secure recovery flows, real-time chat, face verification

Live: database-tulia.vercel.app

Lost Mode: one code, one QR link, one conversation — and the bag comes home.

Bunyala Agri-Climate

Website

A Kenyan social enterprise turning organic waste into protein and fertiliser in the Lake Victoria Basin. We built the platform that carries that story to investors and the public.

Built: content architecture, technical storytelling, public platform

Live: bunyala-agriclimate.org

Waste in. Protein, fertiliser and jobs out.

Decopolis Enterprises

Website

Modern fish cages on Lake Victoria: 120+ tons of Tilapia a year, 40+ local jobs. We built the website that carries their products and sustainability story to buyers.

Built: company website, product presentation, sustainability storytelling

Live: decopolisentreprises.netlify.app

Sustainable fish. Thriving communities. Healthy waters.

Builders, not talkers.

A 20+ person product development company based in Kenya, shipping for clients worldwide.

Why we exist

Plenty of people have ideas. Very few can ship them. Talk is cheap; drawings are easy to find. Building — sensors that survive the field, apps people use daily, platforms that stay up — is the hard part. That gap is where ATIH lives.

We started in 2023 with prototype and IoT work for farms and local enterprises, where a working model in the field beats any presentation. It taught us a habit we never dropped: build the real thing early, test it honestly, improve it with the people who use it.

Three years later we're 20+ people across web, mobile, SaaS and connected hardware. Different scale, same habit.

Mission and vision

Mission

Give any business, anywhere, the kind of product development normally reserved for companies with in-house engineering departments.

Vision

A world where a good idea becomes a working product — no matter who holds it or where they live.

What we hold ourselves to

Build things that work

Not demos that die after the meeting. Products that survive real users, real weather and real networks.

Plain words, honest timelines

What a build takes, what it won't do, what it will cost. If something is a bad idea, you hear it from us first.

Own the outcome

Launch is the middle of the work, not the end. What we ship stays our responsibility.
